Growth Commands

This page lists commands for growing your channel and climb higher in the leaderboard. And the ultimate goal: THE TOP 50

Rules

  • Inappropriate content will result in ignore, warnings or THE HELL DEMENSION
  • Videos can only have a maximum 5 per day
  • Shorts can only have a maximum 8 per day
  • Posts can only have a maximum 20 per day
  • 3 new livestreams per day and 3 livestreams at once
  • Other uploading commands: all 4 per day
  • If you want to post a short that is the same as a video you have posted, reply that video then "!short <>"
